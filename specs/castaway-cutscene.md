# Castaway cutscene

- Show the playable cell with its bed, bars, door and nearby stairs. Living crew enter through the door, find the player on the floor and stop to inspect them before the shot cuts outside.
- Keep all four days and nights. The player briefly looks out during daylight and after dark, then ducks back into the barrel.
- The barrel bobs with a visible waterline across its lower boards. A wake appears beside it, and the storm still rocks it harder.
- At the island, the barrel stops where the waves meet the sand. The opening tips toward land and the player crawls through it onto the wet sand while the barrel stays at the water's edge.
- Castaway still plays to the ending panel, can be replayed, and shows the final shore view in reduced-motion mode.
